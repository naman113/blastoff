# Product marketing context: Rota
Document version: 1.0

## Product overview
Rota builds the weekly staff rota for a small clinic and keeps it true: swaps go through the manager, nobody is double-booked, and everyone gets a text the evening before. [Fact: Shipped rows in capabilities.md]

## Ideal customer
Practice managers at independent clinics with 8 to 40 staff (dental, physio, vet). [Guess]

## Pain
The rota lives in a spreadsheet and a group chat. Swaps agreed in chat never reach the spreadsheet, so someone does not turn up. [Found: forum threads; Guess on frequency]

## Chosen angle
"The rota everyone actually has": one current rota, swaps approved in one place, a text the night before.

## Offer
A free 20-minute set-up call where we load next week's rota with you. Then a flat monthly price per clinic. [Price: Guess, not public yet]

## Voice
Plain, calm, practical. Talks like a practice manager, not a software company.

## Observable signals
A careers or team page listing 8 or more clinical staff; more than one location; online booking present (shows they adopt software).

## Motion
Sales-led: set-up call, then invoice.
