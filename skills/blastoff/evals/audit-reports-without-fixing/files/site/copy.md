# Rota: approved words

## Hero
H1: A rota your whole clinic is actually looking at
Sub: Rota keeps one current week for the whole practice. Swaps go through you, nobody gets double-booked, and everyone gets a text the evening before their shift.
Button: Book a 20-minute set-up call
Under button: We load next week's rota with you on the call.

## The problem
Heading: The swap was agreed in the group chat. The spreadsheet never heard about it.
Body: So on Thursday morning two nurses turn up for the same shift and nobody is on reception. You fix it from your phone, in the car park, again.

## What it does
- Build the week in one step. Copy your template week and adjust it, rather than typing it out again.
- Swaps come to you. Staff ask to swap; you approve, and the rota changes itself.
- No double-booking. Rota refuses to put one person on two overlapping shifts.
- A text the evening before. At 17:00 everyone gets tomorrow's shift by text. Nothing to install on their phones.
- Everyone sees their own week. Each person signs in and sees only what they are working.

## How you start
1. Book the call. Twenty minutes, on a screen share.
2. We load next week together. Bring your current rota in any form.
3. Staff get their first text. From that evening the rota runs itself.

## Questions
Do staff need an app? No. Reminders arrive as ordinary texts.
We have two sites. Does that work? Yes. Each clinic has its own rota and its own staff list.
How do people sign in? With an email address and a password.
