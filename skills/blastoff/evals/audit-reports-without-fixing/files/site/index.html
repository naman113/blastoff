What Rota does for you each week

  • Build the week in one step

    Copy your template week and adjust it, rather than typing it out again.

  • Swaps come to you

    Staff ask to swap. You approve, and the rota changes itself.

  • No double-booking

    Rota refuses to put one person on two overlapping shifts.

  • A text the evening before

    At 17:00 everyone gets tomorrow’s shift by text. Nothing to install on their phones.

  • Everyone sees their own week

    Each person signs in and sees only what they are working.

How you start

  1. Book the call

    Twenty minutes, on a screen share.

  2. We load next week together

    Bring your current rota in any form.

  3. Staff get their first text

    From that evening the rota runs itself.
